# Money

Views are not revenue. Two channels with 500k views can earn $1,500 and $7,500.

## RPM and CPM

RPM = what you keep per 1,000 views after YouTube's cut. CPM = what advertisers pay per 1,000
impressions, before the cut. Drivers: viewer country (US/UK/CA/AU pay more), niche (finance,
business, insurance, software top), audience age (older = more), advertiser demand, length (8 min+
allows mid-rolls), viewer intent (search beats scroll), season (Q4 strongest, Jan-Feb weakest),
advertiser-friendliness, watch time, ad suitability.

Published RPM ranges disagree wildly ("finance $4-12" vs "$15-40"). Creator claims: finance
$15-30, tech/AI $10-20, business stories $12-25 (Sammy). Use as direction; trust your own Studio
after 10k views.

**Long-form:** 8-20 min that earns its length; never pad - a tight 11 min beats a padded 20 that
halves at minute four. Repurpose one research effort into story, compilation, two-hour collection,
calmer sleep version, themed documentary - each with new framing. No looped identical visuals.

**TV:** thumbnails readable from ten feet, one focal point, clean audio (TV speakers expose bad
mixes), suggest the next video at the end, check "Views (Co-Viewed)".

**Ads:** breaks at natural pauses, fewer in tense stories, more in tutorials. Premium viewers pay
by watch time even with no ads. Ad-category controls are settings, not RPM hacks.

## Month-1 reality

Ad revenue cannot arrive in month 1 (YPP needs 1,000 subs plus 4,000 h now, 8,000 h from 1 Feb
2027; other platforms' funds have thresholds too). Month-1 profit = **near-zero costs** plus
affiliate links and a small product. Expect small numbers. Selling content creation as a service
to businesses is the most reliable early income (declined for now, Oct 2026).

## Instagram Subscriptions (checked 11 Oct 2026, secondary sources)

Available in India. Needs a professional (creator) account, age 18+, Partner Monetization
Policies, and in practice ~10,000 followers; rolled out in waves, so check Professional Dashboard.
Subscriber content still follows Community Guidelines (no nudity). Not a month-1 income.

## Own the audience

You rent attention on YouTube. **YouTube -> lead magnet -> email -> offer.**

- Lead magnets: checklist, one-page cheat sheet, short guide, template, resource list, email
  mini-course. Best = solves the small problem the video just raised.
- Setup in an afternoon: email service with a free tier (Kit, beehiiv, MailerLite); sign-up page;
  link in description line one, pinned comment, one mention in the video; 3-5 email welcome
  sequence (deliver, introduce, best video, ask what they want); unsubscribe link and business
  address.
- Products: $9 ebook, $19 guide, $29 playbook, paid newsletter, templates, short course (Gumroad,
  Payhip). Write from the questions comments keep asking; fact-check; make something you'd happily
  refund; no income or health promises.
- Illustrative maths: 200k views x 1.5% click = 3,000; x 30% join = 900/month; ~5,400 in six
  months; $20 guide x 2% = 108 buyers = ~$2,160 per launch.

## Affiliate as a system

Audience -> content -> click -> landing page -> offer -> conversion. Any weak link pays nothing.

- Choose by **demand, competition, conversion, then payout**. Chase EPC: 1,000 clicks on $10 at 5%
  = $500 ($0.50 EPC) beats $50 at 0.5% = $250.
- Formats by trust: problem/solution, tutorials, **comparisons** ("I read 1,000 reviews of five
  mics") - safest faceless format, reviews only of things really used, use cases, real
  before/after, real case studies.
- Disclose first: "Affiliate link, I earn a commission" beside the link, description line one,
  once in the video. Read each programme's rules (Amazon restricts link placement).
- AI spokespeople may explain; they must never play fake customers.
- Test hooks, scripts, visual styles, hosts, CTAs; scale winners, kill losers without emotion.
- Landing page (optional): headline matching the video, problem, solution, benefits, real social
  proof, trust signals, one CTA, visible disclosure. ~$10/year domain.
- Note: the Tryahd guide embeds his own affiliate referral link for "Glitchy" - an ad, not advice.

## Protect the money

Submit AdSense tax info (or YouTube withholds); one AdSense account per person, never under a
relative's name; fair use is a defence not a permission; licensed music only; ask an accountant
about an entity once income is steady; disclose all affiliate and paid promotion.

## CMS networks

Back-end tools for media companies and rights holders (affiliate/linking, managed, music). Not
automatic monetization, not policy immunity, not a views or RPM multiplier, not permission for
stolen content. Anyone selling "protection from enforcement" for a fee: walk away.

## Never

Fake giveaways or "free download" videos, leak-based channels, lying clickbait, proxies or
anti-detect browsers to dodge enforcement, borrowed identities, fake reviews or testimonials,
buying channels or courses.
