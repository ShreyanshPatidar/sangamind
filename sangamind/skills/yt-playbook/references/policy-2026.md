# YouTube rules and changes, 2026

As reported by Tryahd's guide, current to 8 Oct 2026. Check the Help Center before acting on money.

## Timeline

- **15 Jul 2025** - "repetitious content" renamed **"inauthentic content"**. Mass-produced, generic,
  repetitive or manipulative content cannot earn. Reviewed per channel, not per video. YouTube's
  own examples: narrated stories with only superficial differences, slideshows with the same
  narration. It does NOT require a face, and does NOT ban AI or AI voices.
- **Jan 2026** - CEO letter makes "managing AI slop" a priority. Kapwing counted 16 of the top 100
  AI-slop channels gone (11 deleted, 5 emptied): ~35M subscribers, ~4.7B views. ~1 in 5 videos
  recommended to a new account was slop.
- **Mar 2026** - mobile pop-up test asks viewers if a video "feels like AI slop" (5-point scale,
  weighting unknown). Reports that visible human hosts are favoured; no official rule against
  faceless channels.
- **May 2026** - photorealistic AI video is **labelled automatically**. Under the player on long
  videos, overlay on Shorts; can't be removed if made with YouTube's AI tools or C2PA-marked. The
  label alone does not cut reach or monetization (Rene Ritchie). Face-based removal requests
  widened to adults anywhere.
- **10 Aug 2026 announcement, effective 1 Feb 2027** - new YPP applicants need **1,000 subs + 8,000
  public watch hours (365 days)** or **20M Shorts views (90 days)**. Today: 4,000 h / 10M.
  Existing partners keep YPP, but need **10M qualified Shorts views per rolling 90 days** to keep
  Shorts ad revenue (long-form unaffected; switches back on when regained). 500-sub fan-funding
  tier unchanged.
- **27 Aug 2026** - Amazon added to YouTube Shopping affiliate (US; YPP + US Shopping affiliate +
  linked Amazon Associates). Tagged videos get 2x+ product clicks vs description links.
- **Sep 2026** - paid promotion rules become the "Branded Content" policy; label may be added
  automatically, self-disclosure still required.
- **Aug-Oct 2026** - ad guidelines: clearer rules for death in educational/documentary/news;
  October: public-interest content (congressional proceedings, debates on government action) can
  earn.
- **1 Oct 2026** - Shorts feed prioritises original content; channels re-uploading others' clips
  lose reach. Voice-overs, minor edits and template bulk changes do NOT count as adding something.
  Commentary, analysis, own storytelling and own footage do. Shorts cut from your own long videos
  are fine.
- **Jul 2026** - Nielsen: YouTube 14.2% of US TV viewing, the top streamer. Sep: "Views
  (Co-Viewed)" metric in Analytics Advanced Mode.

## Disclosure

Must disclose: a real person made to say/do what they didn't, altered footage of a real event, a
realistic scene that never happened. Need not disclose: AI help with scripts, ideas, captions.
Health, news, elections and finance show the label more prominently. **When unsure, disclose.**

## Watch-hour maths

Hours = views x average minutes watched / 60. At 8 min average, 4,000 h is ~30,000 views; 8,000 h is
~60,000. One 8-minute view equals sixteen 30-second views. Retention on long videos is the cheapest
route into YPP from Feb 2027. **Short (1-5 min) content alone is a slow route to 8,000 hours** -
plan income that does not wait for YPP.

## The six decisions everything hangs off

1. An angle only this channel would have.
2. Visible human judgment: script choices, edits, a consistent voice or persona, stated opinions.
3. Varied formats - no single skeleton.
4. Disclose realistic AI; never impersonate a real person.
5. Own something off-platform: email list, product, affiliate stack.
6. Weight long-form and search; Shorts are the advertising department.

## The five-upload test

Put the last five uploads side by side. If a stranger could swap the titles and nobody would
notice, the channel has a template problem.
