# Multi-platform, 1-5 minute content

## Each platform's job

- **TikTok** - volume and discovery. **Banned in India since 2020.** Shreyansh chose to run it
  over a VPN (11 Oct 2026). Risks to manage: TikTok's terms ban faking your location, so the
  account can be banned and unpaid earnings lost; Creator Rewards pays only residents of eligible
  countries; SIM, IP or device mismatches get accounts flagged. Treat it as reach, not income.
  Indian substitutes: Facebook Reels, Snapchat Spotlight, Moj,
  Josh, Threads.
- **YouTube Shorts** - longevity and discovery; feeds long-form.
- **Instagram Reels** - conversion and audience building.
- **YouTube long-form** - the business (watch hours, mid-rolls at 8 min+, TV audience).

Create once, adapt per platform's native style, distribute. Shorts cut from your own long videos
count as original. Re-uploads of other people's clips lose reach (1 Oct 2026).

## 1-5 minute format notes

- Under 60 s qualifies as Shorts everywhere; 1-3 min works on Shorts (up to 3 min), Reels and
  TikTok; 3-5 min is long-form on YouTube and needs strong retention to count.
- 150 words per minute: 150-750 words. Re-hook every 20-30 s.
- 5-minute videos alone build watch hours slowly (8,000 h = ~96,000 full 5-min views); pair with
  some 8-20 min anchors once a format proves itself.
- Captions on from second one (sound-off viewing); first 3 seconds carry the hook.

## Language

English default with YouTube auto-dubbing / multi-language audio and translated titles reaches
Hindi viewers on YouTube only; Reels translation is partial, TikTok mostly none. Dubbing reaches
but doesn't attract - discovery runs on title and thumbnail language. For money content, teach
universal skills (budgeting, compounding, spending psychology, scams, negotiating), not
country-specific products (401k vs SIP), so one video works in both languages.

## Publishing everywhere (tooling)

- **Buffer or Metricool MCP** - publish one post to all platforms with a single command (from the
  cindiezhu "Jarvis" reel). Buffer's free tier covers a few channels. Any publish step still needs
  Shreyansh's approval - the yt-* gate ("ship it, or change it?") holds.
- **Meta official Ads MCP** - only once running paid promotion.
- Other "Jarvis" ideas from the same reel: give Claude Code a reference image to build a stats
  dashboard; voice mode, or Hermes Agent with Fish Audio for a British voice; Claude in Chrome for
  browser access; RevenueCat MCP for app revenue; FAQs in a markdown file for customer replies;
  custom sub-agents with skills; a daily brief as a routine.

## Tool notes (11 Oct 2026)

- **Thumbnails:** Ideogram (free tier) handles text inside images better than most generators.
- **Shorts from long videos:** OpusClip can auto-cut; `/yt-cut` picks the moments from a transcript for free.
- **Voice:** Kokoro (local, free, commercial use allowed) by default; ElevenLabs via its official MCP only on a paid plan
  (free plan is non-commercial).
