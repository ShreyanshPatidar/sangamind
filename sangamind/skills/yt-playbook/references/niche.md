# Choosing a niche

You need enough interest to survive video 60, and a business. Score, don't vibe.

## The scorecard (1-5 each, 5 always good, out of 65)

1. Demand 2. Competition (5 = few strong rivals) 3. Audience size 4. Evergreen (views in two
years?) 5. Trend potential 6. RPM 7. Affiliate opportunities 8. Digital-product opportunities
9. Production ease (5 = easy faceless) 10. Copyright safety 11. Monetization safety (5 = low risk
of limited ads) 12. Topic count (can you name 100 videos now?) 13. Audience purchasing power

Worked example (illustrative scores, not truth): retirement money 55+ = 53, true crime = 43, game
news (GTA 6) = 40. Game news is a big short spike and a poor long business; retirement money is
quieter and sturdier.

What matters is the intersection: **demand + monetization + production + sustainability**. Winning
three and failing one quietly drains you. Decent on all four carries you.

**Fishnet test:** run 2-3 niches for two months at the same quality bar and let viewers pick.
Don't launch twenty channels before one is validated.

- **Story and mystery:** true crime, cold cases, unsolved mysteries, urban legends, ghost stories,
  conspiracy theories, ocean mysteries, space mysteries, historical/natural/sports disasters,
  animal attacks, dangerous places, abandoned places and mansions, lost civilizations, ancient
  technologies.

  startup failures, tech companies and failures, luxury mansions, celebrity homes, superyachts,
  private jets, luxury cars/watches/hotels, expensive restaurants and objects.
- **Money and self-improvement (high CPM, buyer audiences):** stock market, investing, real estate,
  crypto, passive income, side hustles, productivity, habits, memory, mental models, cognitive
  biases, stoicism, psychology, body language.
- **Science and space (evergreen):** space, Mars, black holes, astronomy, quantum mechanics,
  physics, discoveries, future tech, AI, AI automation, deep sea, rare/extinct/dangerous animals,
  wildlife, animal rescues.
- **Entertainment and culture:** celebrity stories, Hollywood, music industry, movie theories, TV
  breakdowns, anime, gaming history, meme history, internet culture, behind the scenes, athlete
  stories, sports controversies, extreme sports.
- **Food, travel, lifestyle, tech:** street food, weird foods, food history and science, nutrition,
  hidden destinations, fitness, workouts, bodybuilding, recovery, performance, cybersecurity,
  software, gadgets, technology, tech conspiracies, innovation.
- **Live in 2026:** world politics/geopolitics, personal finance for retirees, AI news explained,
  game launches and news, product comparisons and buying guides, immigration and visa explainers,
  sleep and relaxation long-form.

## Risk flags

- **Copyright-heavy:** movie theories, TV, anime, music - borrowed clips need real transformation.
- **Legal:** celebrities, billionaires, company collapses - documented facts, "reportedly", never
  invented quotes.
- **Misinformation-prone:** conspiracies - explain why a theory spread and what evidence says.
- **Health:** explain research; never tell individuals what to take.
- **Graphic:** attacks, disasters - advertisers limit ads; documentary treatment is friendlier.
- **Kids:** some anime/gaming drifts into "made for kids" - personalised ads and features off.
- **Finance (India):** SEBI restricts unregistered finfluencers giving investment advice. Teach how
  things work; never recommend specific stocks or funds or promise returns.

## The author's pick: world politics

Self-renewing topics, stakes built in, adult global audience, weak competition (Wikipedia rewrites
over stock footage). Stay safe: analysis over provocation, primary sources, multiple perspectives,
documented claims only, never fabricated quotes, fair thumbnails, fact vs opinion marked ("the
report shows" / "I think"), verify before publishing, no realistic AI imagery of real politicians,
a source log per video.
