# Recurring AI characters (short-form video)

From two X articles on Jean Phil (k1rallik, 0xAIGOAT, 8-10 Oct 2026) plus checks of the tools named.
Applies to Naina, Mei and Rayna. Rules in [ai-host.md](ai-host.md) still win.

## Why one character beats many pretty faces

Jean Phil (@jean_philanthrope, first posts 17 Sep 2026) reached a 5.6M-view park clip in 3 days,
13.8M on a kitchen clip, and 240K Instagram followers on 11 posts in 3 weeks (the articles' figures).
Three mechanics:

- **One-frame recognition.** 2-4 loud traits that survive any angle: hair, one facial feature, one
  outfit, one repeated gesture. If he can't be described in one sentence, nobody describes him to a
  friend. "Pretty and generic" is the cow nobody looks at (Seth Godin, *Purple Cow*).
- **Same character, new everyday setting.** No backstory needed. The character is the constant; the
  location and ending change.
- **Real-looking motion.** His movement is borrowed from real videos (motion transfer).

The articles credit "is he real or AI?" arguments with part of the spread. **We don't use that:** our
bios say AI and the platform label is on (rule 2 of the Channels README). The articles themselves say to
write "AI CHARACTER" in the bio and make the reveal the hook.

## The build

1. **Design for recognition**: the one-sentence description and a signature gesture.
2. **Lock identity with a character sheet**: portrait, full body, side, three-quarter. Generate from the
   sheet every time, never from text again, or the face drifts by clip 3. (Naina's 14 references are
   this sheet; Mei and Rayna need theirs.)
3. **Research the feed**: save 1M+ clips with one person and a still camera, and ask "does my character
   make this better?". Study the idea, film our own scene (see "Where we differ").
4. **Animate**: start with 5-8 s clips, one person, still camera, visible hands. Plan on about 4 takes per
   finished clip. Check mouth, hair flicker, hands and clothes; regenerate if they're off.
5. **Publish 10 clips over 10-14 days**, same name and avatar everywhere. Watch completion, shares and
   profile visits. The only question: do viewers want another episode? **Don't automate before
   this proof.**

## Tools for identity and longer clips (checked 12 Oct 2026, prices move)

| Tool | Does | Cost (sources conflict) |
|---|---|---|
| Higgsfield **AI Influencer** (launched 2 Oct 2026) | Builds a character from 19 controls, returns portrait and full body; 5 free generations at launch | ~$0.05-0.06 per character |
| Higgsfield **Soul ID** | Trained on 20-80 images of one character: the long-term identity lock | Plan credits |
| Higgsfield **Genjutsu** | Motion transfer and object/character swap into a reference video | 15 s: ~$2 (480p), ~$5.20 (720p), ~$7.20 (1080p) |
| Kling 3.0 (Character ID / Motion Control) | 3-5 reference images to an identity embedding | Up to 15 s a clip |
| Seedance 2.5 | Up to 30 references and **30 s in one pass** | ~$0.32 a second in the article's budget |
| Arcads | Talking AI actors for product ads (stock actors; custom actor from your image on Pro only); same actor and voice across clips; up to ~2 min | ~$110-220/month, about $11 a clip (bills per full minute) |
| Nano Banana 2 / Pro, ChatGPT Images | Stills and character sheets (GPT Go covers ChatGPT Images) | ~$0.07 an image |

No tool guarantees an identical face at every angle, light and outfit. A 30 s clip is usually stitched
from shorter takes using first and last frames.

**First-test budget (articles):** 10 clips × 4 takes × 8 s = 320 s of video ≈ $102, plus images, music and
retries, so **$155-300**, plus 30-50 hours the first time. That breaks the README's "₹0" cost row for
any character that moves, so it's a decision for Shreyansh, not a default.

**Arcads vs our channels:** it makes a stock or Pro-only custom actor *talk to camera* about a product.
Naina doesn't talk, and Mei and Rayna would need the Pro tier to be themselves, so it's the wrong tool
for the channels. Its real use is later, as a money route: making labelled AI ads for brands as a
service (same "no fake customers" rule as UGC in `sources.md`).

## Where we differ from the articles

- **No face-swapping our character into other people's viral videos.** That reuses their footage
  (copyright, and the 1 Oct 2026 originality penalty). Motion transfer from a clip **Shreyansh films
  himself**, or a licensed one, is fine.
- **No ambiguity bait.** Labelled as AI, always.
- **No tokens or memecoins.** The $235K was wallet fees, not audited profit. "Launching a coin does not
  create demand, and market cap is not your money" (the article's own warning). Money routes we keep:
  brand deals, affiliate links for the tools, Instagram Subscriptions.
- **Don't clone Jean Phil or Derek Mercer.** Copy number 1,000,001 gets nothing.
